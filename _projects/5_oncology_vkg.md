An ongoing, multi-institutional effort to build **image-grounded knowledge graphs for abdominal oncology** — 3D-grounded clinical knowledge graphs supporting explainable surgical decision support across liver, pancreas, and multi-organ abdominal imaging.

### Key Idea

Medical image segmentations carry rich anatomical and topological structure that gets lost when models output only masks or labels. This project converts abdominal CT segmentations into **ontology-grounded knowledge graphs** that link imaging phenotypes to structured clinical context, so downstream reasoning is explainable and evidence-grounded.

### Outputs So Far

- *From Perception to Reasoning: Image-Grounded Knowledge Graphs for Topology-Aware Medical Analysis in Abdominal CT* — **CVPR 2026 MedReasoner Workshop**
- *Ontology-Grounded Imaging Phenotype Knowledge Graphs for Explainable Oncology Reasoning from Abdominal CT* — **AMIA 2026**
- Vision-KG work for surgical decision support — under review at **AAAI 2027**
