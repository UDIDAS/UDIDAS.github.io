Part of **Project GENESIS**, a collaboration with **Children's Mercy Hospital** on digital-twin modeling of the pediatric airway.

### My Role

I build the **4D (3D + temporal) segmentation-driven multimodal knowledge graph** layer: converting dynamic airway segmentations into structured, queryable representations of airway anatomy over time, so the digital twin can support clinical decision-making rather than just visualization.

### Status

Early stage — a companion methods paper, *Multimodal Knowledge Graphs for 4D Airway Segmentation and Digital-Twin Modeling*, is under review at **WACV**.
